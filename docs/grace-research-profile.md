# Research sample profiles

Candidate internal deterministic route for questions such as “从背调系统分析目标客户和国家画像” and “肯尼亚国家画像”. Uses only the existing research aggregate loader: fixed country/category labels and country-category cells with at least five records. No company identities, contacts, source narrative, new endpoint, authority or model call.

Distinguish index records from deduplicated buyers, and sample coverage from demand, buyer quality or country attractiveness. Category-role and preparation suggestions are hypotheses requiring verification. Keep source date separate from retrieval date (Shanghai); missing, invalid, future or over-48-hour dates cannot establish current targets. Failed reads remain unavailable. Country list is explicitly bounded to twelve groups/page; no suppressed cells inferred or filled with zero. Guest voice requests cannot access the route; the existing owner, signed voice, audit and rate gates remain.

Validation: synthetic routing, disclosure, country filter, paging, date/failure and injection boundaries plus full regression. Real owner question and refresh acceptance are still required before claiming the feature is production-verified. No migration or permission change; rollback is the normal reviewed revert and matching function deployment, preserving audit and source records.

The current aggregate does not contain verified purchase needs, exact product demand, budget, procurement cycle, channel/service capability or conversion linkage. This route cannot produce evidence-backed individual company fit or commercial country scores without a separately scoped source interface and verification.

Depth extension: country-specific entry hypotheses use only disclosed country/category intersections, never global categories substituted as local demand. Separate known record labels, hypotheses and unknown procurement evidence. Category-specific questions, stop conditions and product-verification gates are suggestions, not automatic lead ratings or supply commitments. Ignore original unverified fitScore. Each country displays all disclosed category counts; deeper playbooks cover at most the first two by record count with explicit remaining coverage.

Region filters cover the explicit Southeast Asia/Africa request, with twelve-country paging; no default region is silently selected. Query-only `背调画像：` context supports bounded depth/country/page followups, preserves scope and resets it for explicit replacements. It carries no source answer text; unrelated and action requests do not inherit this route.
